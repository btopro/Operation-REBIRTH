# src/stage/ — stages

Every stage: layout, art, hazards (and their tunables), music, how it is unlocked.
The root `AGENTS.md` (rules, stack, map) applies too. Keep this file up to date when you change what it describes.

## Stages
- `src/stage/<name>Stage.js`, list in `stages.js` (each entry also names its `music` / `musicList`; `trackList()` = the TRACK picker on the stage select screen: AUTO / RANDOM / any stage's music / owned shop tracks -> `settings.track`, played by `MatchScene.pickedTrack`); `backroomsStage.js` swaps between 4 levels every minute;
- `infectedStage.js` (INFECTED.EXE: virus pop-ups - `IDIOT` block tunes YOU ARE AN IDIOT);
- `aeroStage.js` (AERO MEADOW: Frutiger Aero + overgrown gadgets, CD SHOWER);
- `baseplateStage.js` (BASEPLATE 2012: an original homage to 2010-2016 block-building games - no real names/logos; ROCKET BARRAGE, LOOSE BRICKS, LAG SPIKE = `match.frozen` then everyone snaps back to their position a second ago, THE GRIEFER (a code-drawn blocky troublemaker on a tower top throwing classic gear - `GRIEFER` tunables, `step_griefer`; drawn crisp by the shared `avatar(g, x, y, dir, armA, alpha, look)`), THE HACKER (hooded exploiter: fly-through flings, swaps two fighters' places, gets BANNED - `HACKER` tunables, chat `HACK_CHAT`, `step_hacker`, `drawHacker`); sliding brick `BRICK` carries fighters, jump pads `PAD`; background guests are Mason's drawings (`tools/sprites/art/baseplate_guest*.png` -> `baseplate_guests.py` -> one 2-frame sheet `guests.png`, key `bp_guests`), tunables `GUEST` (1 in 50 glitched); music is a rotation of his four tracks (pixelated_audio 4, 5, 6, 10) via `stage.musicList` -> `playPlaylist` in music.js).
- `fracturedStage.js` (FRACTURED: Mason's GALAXY FRAGMENT gem grown into a floating crystal island in deep space - `GPAL` from his sprite; SHARD RAIN / CRYSTAL SURGE, tunables `RAIN` / `SURGE`; music `bgm_fractured` from `tools/audio/fractured_music.py`; its entry has `shop: 'ext-fragment'` = locked until bought - `stageLocked()` in stages.js, padlocked on stage select, never RANDOM, no track in the TRACK list until owned).
- `stalingradStage.js` (STALINGRAD: a ruined city in the winter of 1942 - the river, a wrecked factory, a hanging girder; hazards ARTILLERY (shells whistle in on a warning marker), SNIPER (a glint far off tracks someone, locks on, fires), COLLAPSE; music `bgm_shop_soviet`; locked by `shop: 'ext-vodka'` = the VODKA bottle from the PAPERSPLEASE shop).
- `willowStage.js` (13 WILLOW LANE: an original 90s slasher homage - no real film, killer or mask: a grey two-storey house on a stormy night in 1996, lawn + porch floor, porch-roof / balcony / dormer ledges, rain, lightning that sometimes shows him in the upstairs window (`STORM`), a TV glowing through the window, a ringing phone on the porch; THE CALLER = a doll-faced killer in a green rain slicker and rain hat, drawn by `killer(g, x, y, dir, pose, alpha, step)`; hazards THE CALL (`CALL`: the phone rings, the door bursts open, he charges across the lawn), BEHIND YOU (`BEHIND`: fades in behind one fighter, then stabs), THE STALK (`STALK`: walks the lawn slashing anyone near); music `bgm_slasher` from `tools/audio/slasher_music.py`; phone ring = sfx `phone`; locked by `secret: '13'` = the shopkeeper's code 13 - `stageLocked` checks found codes through `setSecretCheck` (achievements.js hands it over; stages.js can't import achievements.js back)).
- `desertStage.js` (DUST RUNNER: you fight on the roof of a 1980s Class A motorhome drawn after Mason's reference photo - cream ribbed siding, brown / gold / tan stripes top and middle wrapping round a rounded front cap, raked windscreen, black grille with square headlights, dark-framed windows (one tall, one with grid panes), beige storage hatches, flared white wheel arches, roof air-con units, a satellite dish, a rail rack with a cooler and jerry can, a mud-flap fringe (no real plate or text); roof + rail rack + air-con unit are the platforms; the body is drawn once (`rvBody`), the moving bits every tick (`rv`: wheels, exhaust, blinking light, dust); the world scrolls past (mesas, cacti, telephone poles, road dashes); falling off lands you on the road (`platforms[3]`, `road: true`), which drags you backwards fast (`ROAD.drag`, scroll `ROAD.scroll`) towards the left blast zone, so you get one jump (or a ledge grab) to get back; being run over by any vehicle is an instant KO (`runOver()` -> `match.onKO`): on the road in front of the motorhome's nose (`BUMPER.reach`) or of the DEA van's front (`DEA.ram.reach`). Two hazards, taking turns: THE DEA VAN (kind `dea`) (`VAN` = its size/layout, `DEA` = the attack, `van()` draws it - dark navy, strobes, light bar, DEA in plain yellow letters (no badge or seal), a sliding side door; `agent()` draws the agents - navy windbreaker, vest with a yellow panel, cap, glasses, poses aim / throw / launcher / stand): it pulls up behind, the side door slides open, two agents aim laser sights then fire bursts of tracers and throw bouncing grenades, then a third climbs out of the roof hatch with a rocket launcher (`ROCKET`, 20%); its front runs you over; and THE DEA HELICOPTER (kind `heli`, `HELI` tunables, `heli()` draws it - navy, bubble windscreen, open side door, DEA on the tail boom, rotor blur, strobes): flies in over the roof, a door gunner (in a riot helmet - `agent(..., helmet)`) sweeps a laser sight and fires bursts, then agents fast-rope down (2, or 3 with 3+ fighters, at most `TROOP.max` on the roof) and stay after it leaves - `this.troops`, `stepTroops` / `drawTroop`: SHIELD agents (riot shield blocks hits from the front, shield bash) and KNIFE agents (quick slashes, hop up after you); fighters hit them through `strikeTargets` (they build %, light - `TROOP.weight`), knocked off they're dragged away or run over; after `TROOP.life` they bail off the back. A hazard every ~17-23 s. Music `bgm_desert` (`tools/audio/desert_music.py`); locked by `secret: 'HEISENBERG'`).
- `maskedStage.js` (MASKED THRONE, from Mason's dark-statue picture: a faceless stone king in the CROWN OF MASKS (`KING` = where/how big, drawn round 0,0 then placed; its sword is its own graphics `swordG`, hidden while thrown), studded slate floor, pillars, the king's stone palm; THE STAGE IS DARK - `drawDark` paints a 160x90 canvas `masked_dark` every tick (dark, with light punched out round fighters / the brazier / seams) stretched over the screen on the UI camera (so it doesn't show in the title demo or the sim, where the UI camera is off), tunables `DARK`; hazards THE KING'S SWORD (`SWORD`), COMEDY & TRAGEDY (`MASKS`: heal under comedy, damaging tears under tragedy), LIGHTS OUT (`LIGHTS`); locked by `shop: 'ext-crown'`; music `bgm_shop_noli`). FULL AERO on INFECTED.EXE draws a clash instead of the plain aero sky (`renderAeroClash` in MatchScene). Select grid: `STAGE_SLOTS` in stages.js

Hazards use `HazardCycle` (`hazardCycle.js`): stage defines `start_<kind>` / `step_<kind>`.
