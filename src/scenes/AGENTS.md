## Arrival intros
Files: `src/scenes/fighterIntros.js` (`FighterIntros`, made by MatchScene when there's a countdown - not in training/title demo/sim)

Each fighter arrives on stage during the 3-2-1 countdown, one after another (`TIME`: length, arrival point, stagger). `INTROS` = each fighter's arrival, `SKIN_INTROS` keyed `id:COSTUME NAME` = the costume's own one (the high-quality costumes all have one). Kinds: rise / drop / swarm / portal / warp / bubble / flicker / bolt / iris / slash, plus particle shapes (`shape()`); options color/color2/sfx/pre sfx + per-kind flags (lure, rave, ...). `show()` hides/fades all of a fighter's display objects (sprite, fx, tag, WOW's head) until they arrive.

## Win cutscenes
Files: `src/scenes/WinScene.js`

When Soul Summoner, Mr Wake, Lacy or a test fighter (NU - ends in a mic drop (`micdrop` anim), AERO - purges infections, rises, shines as FULL AERO, screen switches off (`off`), WHAT ONCE WAS - pushes into its TV: I'M FREE NOW! over and over (`tvLines`), a roar (`sfx roar`), the screen cracks, ROSEMARY - turns SEEN) wins, `MatchScene.toResults` plays their backstory cinematic first (`CUTS`: shots, title card, line; `shot_<id>_<name>` draws each shot; losers appear as silhouettes), then Results. Skippable. After the results (or quitting a match) you go back to wherever the match was started (`settings.from`: Setup or TestFighters).
